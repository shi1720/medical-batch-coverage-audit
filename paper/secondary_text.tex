Seed-0 normalization controls on PneumoniaMNIST yield class-flip rates of CNN-BN: 19.6\% full BN, 15.4\% mixed moments, and 15.1\% first-layer-only; ResNet-BN: 45.3\% full BN, 34.4\% mixed moments, and 12.5\% first-layer-only. The fixed 50:50 moment mixture is a control, not a full TTN implementation. Complete secondary results are released, including a matched affine-intensity condition and batch sizes 8 and 32.
