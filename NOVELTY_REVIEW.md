# Literature and novelty assessment

Search date: 11 October 2026. Scope: focused primary-source review, not proof of universal absence or a patentability search. Titles, authors and bibliographic metadata must be checked against the cited primary source. Full-text PDFs inspected where indicated. Existing local manuscripts were extracted and their abstracts/problem statements reviewed; none studies medical-image inference contexts or conformal batch-scheduling coverage. Prior work on shared-label evaluation and joint couplings is distinct; no text, figures, code, proofs or results from those manuscripts is reused.

## Candidate screening

1. Laterality-aware test-time augmentation: rejected as the central proposal. Class-equivariant transformations and disabling laterality-violating flips are established; swapping anatomical labels after a digital reflection can also confuse image orientation with patient anatomy.
2. Class-imbalance-robust test-time batch normalization: rejected as a novel method claim. TTN, DELTA, TRIBE, MedBN and more recent logit-adjusted methods already address batch composition or imbalance.
3. Scheduling-robust conformal coverage: retained as a controlled empirical medical-imaging study. The max-score construction is established robust/simultaneous conformal inference. The contribution sought is a paired audit that changes companions while retaining the query, distinguishes fixed-context from simultaneous coverage, compares calibration remedies and normalization controls, and releases complete executable evidence. It is an incremental empirical contribution, not an industry-defining algorithm or a new general conformal guarantee.

## Closest work and boundaries

- Ioffe & Szegedy, Batch Normalization (ICML 2015), https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167. Establishes the normalization mechanism. We do not discover cross-sample dependence.
- Salimans et al., Improved Techniques for Training GANs (NeurIPS 2016), https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03498. Virtual/reference BN already addresses sample dependence. Pinning a reference cohort is not claimed as new.
- Wang et al., Tent (ICLR 2021), https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10726. Entropy-based parameter adaptation. Our weights/affine parameters do not change at inference, isolating normalization statistics. We do not compare full TTA performance with Tent.
- Lim et al., TTN (ICLR 2023), https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05155. Source/target statistic interpolation is established. Our fixed 0.5 moment mixture is a control, not a reproduction of TTN's learned channel sensitivities.
- Zhao et al., DELTA (ICLR 2023), https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13018. Documents biased normalization and online class imbalance. We do not claim those are new observations.
- Park et al., MedBN (CVPR 2024), https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Park_MedBN_Robust_Test-Time_Adaptation_against_Malicious_Test_Samples_CVPR_2024_paper.html. Full text downloaded and inspected. Malicious companion contamination and robust median normalization are established. Our intervention uses unmodified benchmark companions and evaluates prediction-set inclusion rather than attack success on point predictions.
- Massena et al., Efficient Robust Conformal Prediction via Lipschitz-Bounded Networks (ICML 2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05434. Full text downloaded and inspected. Restrictive/supremum scores and robust calibration are established. Our finite context maximum is exactly computable, but not a new robust-conformal principle.
- Timans et al., Max-Rank (AISTATS 2025), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/timans25a.html. Full text downloaded and inspected. Dependence-aware simultaneous inference is established.
- Schlembach et al., Corrected Max-Rank (COPA 2026), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v329/schlembach26a.html. Full text downloaded and inspected. Includes rank-to-score correction and infinity convention. Implement as a comparator with the next order statistic, not the original off-by-one implementation.
- Gazin et al., Powerful batch conformal prediction for classification (AISTATS 2025), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/gazin25a.html. Protects a vector of labels for different test examples. Our simultaneous event concerns one fixed example under multiple companion contexts, not the joint correctness of every member of a clinical batch.
- Lyu et al., Conformal Uncertainty Indicator for Continual Test-Time Adaptation (2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02998. Adaptive pseudo-label reliability under sequential drift, not frozen-query companion interventions. Must not claim first integration of CP and TTA.
- Yang et al., MedMNIST v2 (Scientific Data 2023), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01721-8. Benchmark source; clinical use is expressly excluded. Source imaging studies are also cited in the manuscript.

Searches included exact combinations of "conformal prediction" with "batch normalization", "batch composition", "inference batch", "batch-dependent", "companion", and "scheduler"; and TTA imbalance, reference normalization, robust conformal and max-rank queries. No directly matching controlled companion-context coverage study was identified in this bounded search. This does not establish firstness. The paper must not say the problem has never been studied, or claim a comprehensive systematic review.

## Publication judgement

The defensible contribution is a reproducible mechanistic benchmark and a concrete coverage target. Acceptance depends on reviewer assessment of the operational relevance and empirical scope. Current weaknesses: mostly 28-pixel inputs, one 224-pixel pretrained-backbone extension, synthetic context scheduling, no patient identifiers, finite context menu, and established calibration corrections. Do not describe the manuscript as guaranteed to be accepted, proven clinically useful, patentable, or a breakthrough.

Additional overlap check: Bozorgtabar et al., LATA, CVPR 2026, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Bozorgtabar_LATA_Laplacian-Assisted_Transductive_Adaptation_for_Conformal_Uncertainty_in_Medical_VLMs_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf; MICCAI 2025 SCP-T https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/0955-Paper4783.html. These already investigate adaptation and conformal uncertainty in medical VLMs. Our study must not claim that combination is new. The remaining focus is the paired, unchanged-query companion intervention and the joint-over-context coverage estimand, evaluated with established simultaneous corrections. Search cannot establish universal absence of prior work; this is an empirical audit with a bounded, incremental claim.

A late search on 12 October 2026 (IST) found ConformalShift: Targeted Event Reordering Against Adaptive ECG Monitoring, Arash Vashagh and Yasmin Vashagh, arXiv:2608.03628 (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03628. Its abstract was inspected: it reorders authentic prior events to manipulate delayed-feedback adaptive thresholds while preserving ECG waveforms, labels, classifier scores, and the event multiset. This is directly relevant evidence that medical conformal systems can be scheduling-sensitive without signal modification. Our paper must not claim that broad observation as new. The distinction is frozen, stateless calibration thresholds and companion-dependent classifier scores through BN, with an all-context event over a fixed menu; not online feedback or an optimized event permutation. Cite this near neighbor explicitly. The retained novelty claim remains a bounded empirical medical-image audit, not a new general scheduling-security principle.
